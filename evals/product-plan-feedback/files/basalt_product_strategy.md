## The Basalt Platform

The platform is architected around three pillars that together deliver end-to-end workflow intelligence:

- **Perceive.** Basalt continuously ingests signals from every connected system, building a real-time model of how work actually happens.
- **Reason.** Our agentic core plans, prioritizes, and sequences work autonomously, applying enterprise context at every step.
- **Act.** Basalt agents execute across systems of record, closing the loop from insight to outcome without friction.

Each pillar compounds the others. Perception feeds reasoning; reasoning drives action; action generates new signal. This flywheel is the engine of workflow intelligence, and no other platform closes the loop.

## Strategic Principles

Four principles govern every product decision we make in 2027:

- **Platform over point solutions.** We do not ship isolated tools; every capability strengthens the whole. Breadth is the strategy.
- **AI-native, not AI-added.** Agentic intelligence is the foundation of the architecture, not a layer bolted onto legacy workflows.
- **Enterprise-grade from day one.** Security, scale, and reliability are table stakes; we engineer for the most demanding environments on earth.
- **Velocity as a moat.** Shipping fourteen flagship capabilities in four quarters is itself a statement no competitor can answer.

These principles are non-negotiable. They are how a category leader behaves before the category has a name.

## 2027 Roadmap

Fourteen flagship capabilities will ship across four release waves. Together they complete the platform vision.

### Wave 1 — Foundation (Q1)

1. Insight Graph: maps every process, document, and decision across the enterprise into a unified, self-updating intelligence layer
2. Universal Connector Fabric: one-click integrations with 300+ enterprise systems, normalized under a single semantic schema
3. Semantic Vault: enterprise-grade knowledge storage that makes all institutional context instantly retrievable by agents
4. Signal Mesh: continuously monitors connected systems and surfaces anomalies before they become problems

### Wave 2 — Intelligence (Q2)

5. Basalt Cortex: our proprietary reasoning engine that plans, sequences, and executes multi-step workflows autonomously
6. Autopilot Actions: agents that complete entire workflows end-to-end without human intervention
7. Process Genome: automatically discovers and models every recurring workflow from observed activity
8. Adaptive Playbooks: self-optimizing automation templates that improve with every execution

### Wave 3 — Experience (Q3)

9. Ambient Copilot: an always-on assistant embedded in every surface of the workday
10. Prism Dashboards: real-time, natural-language-configurable views into any workflow metric
11. Knowledge Loom: weaves fragmented documentation into living, always-current knowledge assets

### Wave 4 — Scale (Q4)

12. Compliance Evidence Collector: automatically gathers SOC 2 audit evidence from connected tools
13. Orchestration Studio: a no-code canvas for composing agents into enterprise-scale automation networks
14. Velocity Engine: a performance layer delivering sub-second agent responses at global scale

Each wave builds on the last. By the close of Wave 4, the platform reaches general availability as a complete, self-reinforcing system.
