# Northstar — Q3 2026 Board Update

---

## Slide 2 — Strategy

Multi-site healthcare employers lose weeks reconciling worker credentials across facilities. Northstar validates each worker once and syncs approval to every facility, so new sites can staff in days instead of weeks.

---

## Slide 3 — Commitments for Q4

1. Restore enterprise implementation time to under 45 days by December 15. Owner: COO.
2. Launch the renewal-risk model across the full customer base by November 1. Owner: CCO.
3. Keep gross revenue retention above 91%. Owner: CCO.

## Slide 5 — Renewal-risk pilot

The pilot covered 42 enterprise accounts. It identified 11 as high risk; CSM intervention retained 7 of the 9 accounts that reached renewal during the quarter. The board asked management to roll the model out if the pilot retained at least 5 accounts.
