# Quarry — 2027 Annual Plan
## Board Meeting, November 2026

---

## Slide 2 — Strategy (unchanged)

Mid-size commercial contractors lose bids because estimating in spreadsheets takes weeks. Quarry cuts estimate time from weeks to days by pricing new bids from the contractor's own past-job costs, so they bid more work and win more of it.

2027 is an execution year: same strategy, more territory.

---

## Slide 3 — 2026 in review

- ARR: $11.3M (plan was $12.0M)
- Gross retention: 92%, flat year over year
- Net retention: 109%
- 2026 finished at 94% of plan; the miss was concentrated in H2 new business.

---

## Slide 4 — Sales traction

- H2 pipeline coverage: 2.1x against a 3.0x target.
- Win rate: 22% in Q3, down from 29% in Q1.
- The main driver is that sales cycles lengthened, from a 68-day median in Q1 to 97 days in Q3.
- The 2027 plan assumes win rate recovers to 26% and cycles return to roughly 80 days.

---

## Slide 5 — Team and organization

- We reorganized go-to-market in September: sales, marketing, and partnerships now report to a single CRO, and the SDR team moved under marketing.
- Headcount plan for 2027: 96 → 121.
- Key open roles: VP Engineering, 9 AEs.

---

## Slide 6 — 2027 budget

- 2027 opex plan: $18.4M, up 22% from 2026.
- Drivers:
  - 9 new AE hires ($2.1M loaded): 40% of H1 inbound demand came from Texas and the Southeast, where we have no field presence today; these hires cover those two territories.
  - Infrastructure +$600K: SOC 2 Type II and data-residency work. 6 of our 10 largest open prospects required both in security review this year; two stalled on it.
  - Everything else grows roughly with headcount.
- The plan reaches EBITDA breakeven in Q4 2027 at 88% of the sales plan.
