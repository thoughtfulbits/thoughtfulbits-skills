# Solace — Security Architecture Decision
## Special board session, October 2026

---

## Slide 2 — Decision

Management recommends building a dedicated EU data plane and asks the board to approve an $850K budget increase for delivery by June 2027.

Decision needed by October 30 so infrastructure reservations can be signed before the November pricing change.

---

## Slide 3 — Why this decision is in front of the board

- 6 of our 10 largest open prospects require EU-only storage and processing.
- Those six prospects represent $3.8M of potential ARR.
- Two existing customers representing $620K ARR have contract renewals in Q3 2027 with the same requirement.
- Our current US data plane cannot meet it contractually.

---

## Slide 4 — Options considered

| Option | Incremental 2027 cost | Earliest availability | Commercial coverage | Principal risk |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| Dedicated EU data plane | $850K | June 2027 | All six prospects and both renewals | Delivery slips beyond renewal window |
| EU storage only | $310K | March 2027 | Two prospects; neither renewal | Buyers still reject US processing |
| Defer | $0 | N/A | None | $4.42M ARR remains at risk |

---

## Slide 5 — Evidence and delivery confidence

- Architecture spike completed with AWS and an external security reviewer.
- Estimate is based on 11 engineering workstreams with named owners.
- Three workstreams remain uncertain; the budget includes a 20% contingency.
- VP Engineering owns delivery. Monthly board email updates begin November 30.

---

## Slide 6 — Downside and trigger

If fewer than three of the six prospects complete security validation by February 15, management will pause the final $280K of implementation spend and return to the board.

---

## Slide 7 — Approval requested

Approve an $850K increase to the 2027 infrastructure and security budget for the dedicated EU data plane. CFO owns the budget amendment; VP Engineering owns delivery.
