# Harbor — Q2 2026 Board Update

---

## Slide 2 — What Harbor does

Restaurant owners lose hours every week fixing payroll errors caused by tip splits and shift swaps. Harbor runs payroll that handles tips and swaps automatically, so a manager closes payroll in 15 minutes instead of an afternoon.

Everything in this deck ties back to that sentence.

## Slide 3 — Q2 numbers

- ARR: $6.8M, up 11% QoQ
- Customers: 1,240 (+92 net new logos)
- NRR: 104%
- Momentum continues to compound across the business.

## Slide 4 — Sales

- New logos: 92 (Q1: 97)
- Win rate: 31%, down from 38% in Q1.
- Why: Gusto launched a restaurant bundle in April with POS-plus-payroll discount pricing. In 14 of the 22 deals we lost since launch, the buyer named the Gusto bundle discount as the deciding factor. We are piloting a counter-bundle with our POS partner (TouchBistro) in 40 active deals; early read expected in 4–6 weeks.

## Slide 5 — Competition

We compete primarily with Gusto and ADP Run in the restaurant segment. We win on product depth for restaurants, and we rarely lose to ADP.

## Slide 6 — Retention

- Monthly logo churn: 2.1%, up from 1.4% in Q1.
- This was driven by an increase in cancellations among customers in their first 90 days.
- We are investing in best-in-class onboarding to address this.

## Slide 7 — Product

**Shipping next quarter: Tip-pooling compliance engine.**
In the last 8 weeks we interviewed 27 customers about compliance. 19 named state tip-pooling rules as their top compliance worry; 11 have been fined in the past year, averaging $4,200 per fine. The engine validates pool configurations against state rules before each pay run. 9 of the 27 said they would move up a tier for this; it is priced into the Q4 plan at +$180K ARR.

**Exploring: Harbor Capital (working-capital lending).**
Embedded finance is a natural expansion for payroll platforms, and analysts project a $30B embedded-lending market for SMBs by 2028. We plan to staff two engineers on a pilot in Q4.
