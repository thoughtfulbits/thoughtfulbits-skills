# Northstar — Q4 2026 Board Update

---

## Slide 2 — Strategy

Multi-site healthcare employers lose weeks reconciling worker credentials across facilities. Northstar validates each worker once and syncs approval to every facility, so new sites can staff in days instead of weeks.

## Slide 3 — Quarter in one page

- ARR: $19.0M, 96% of the $19.8M plan
- Logo retention: 94%
- Net revenue retention: 105%
- Enterprise implementation: 51 days median
- We executed well and enter 2027 with strong momentum.

## Slide 4 — Retention

- Logo retention: 94%, up from 93% last quarter.
- Renewal-risk model is now available to the customer-success team.
- Gross revenue retention is no longer shown because logo retention better reflects customer health.

---

## Slide 5 — Implementation

- Median enterprise implementation time improved from 63 to 51 days.
- We missed the commitment to restore implementation below 45 days by December 15.
- Cause: security-review queues added a median 13 days for enterprise customers; 24 of 31 implementations exceeding 45 days waited on customer security approval.
- Response: CTO owns a standard security packet due January 31. Success measure: fewer than 20% of implementations wait more than 10 days on security review.

---

## Slide 6 — Product

**Renewal-risk model rollout**

The model was made available to all CSMs on December 12. Usage is optional; 38% of CSMs used it in December. We have not measured renewal outcomes outside the pilot yet.
